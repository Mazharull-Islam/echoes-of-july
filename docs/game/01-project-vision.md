# Echoes of July — Project Vision

## Vision

Echoes of July is an interactive documentary built as a narrative web experience that enables players to understand, explore, and reflect upon the events of the July movement through immersive storytelling, historical evidence, and meaningful participation.

The project is designed to preserve historical memory while making the experience accessible to younger generations through interactive media.

---

## Mission

Create an emotionally engaging, historically respectful, and technically polished experience that encourages curiosity, empathy, and reflection rather than passive reading.

---

## Core Principles

1. Story comes before technology.

2. Historical accuracy is prioritized over spectacle.

3. Every interaction should reinforce learning.

4. Simplicity is preferred over unnecessary complexity.

5. One polished experience is more valuable than many unfinished features.

6. Data should drive behavior whenever possible.

---

## Target Audience

• Students

• Young adults

• General audiences interested in modern Bangladeshi history

• Hackathon judges

---

## Project Goals

- Present historical events respectfully.

- Encourage exploration rather than passive reading.

- Build a reusable narrative engine.

- Deliver a polished MVP within hackathon constraints.

---

## Non-Goals

The MVP will NOT attempt to simulate every historical event.

The MVP will NOT include multiplayer.

The MVP will NOT require user accounts.

The MVP will NOT rely on backend infrastructure.
